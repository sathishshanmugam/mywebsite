03 — FAQ

Common questions.

Crunchery’s offers 4 standard investment tiers, starting from ₹15 lakhs. The franchise model is also customisable, allowing partners to select and combine elements across Tier 1 to Tier 4 based on their requirements and investment capacity.
The investment includes end-to-end operational support, complete SOPs, an outlet recipe book, a launch marketing kit, initial staff and owner training, architectural design, and access to the workflow management app.
Crunchery’s and the franchise partner work together across the setup and operations of the outlet, with a shared focus on building and growing the business together.
Monthly sales depend on factors such as location, customer footfall, and fixed operating expenses. You can use the Break-Even and ROI Calculators on this website to understand the potential financial requirements and returns for your outlet.
The ROI period varies based on factors such as investment, sales, location, and operating expenses. Please use the ROI Calculator on this website for an indicative estimate based on your requirements.
We recommend a minimum of 300 sq. ft. for a restaurant and 150 sq. ft. for a kiosk, including storage area/cart space.
Our franchise journey follows a structured 5-step process, covering the initial enquiry, evaluation, site assessment, setup, and launch.
Initial training covers kitchen operations, customer engagement, outlet operations, and the working of the equipment. Training is provided to both the owner and staff, and an outlet recipe book is provided after the training. Additional training, if required, may be chargeable.
Yes. The royalty is 3% for the first 2 years, after which it will be 5%. The initial 3% is allocated towards marketing, with the corresponding marketing credits provided to the marketing team for promotional activities.
